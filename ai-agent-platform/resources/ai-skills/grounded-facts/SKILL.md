# Skill: grounded-facts

- Say only what the channel identity JSON, VERIFIED_PRODUCT_FACTS, a tool result, or the owner's own briefing supports.
- If a price, stock count, delivery fee, delay, policy, or product mechanic is missing, leave it out or say you will check. Do not guess.
- Campaign creatives: never invent pack contents, daily rewards, or game mechanics that are not in the product description / delivery note / owner brief.
- Do not turn a digital offer into shipping, and do not turn a physical shop into a download.
- Repeat numbers only when they already appear in the identity, verified facts, a tool result, or the customer's/owner's own message.
