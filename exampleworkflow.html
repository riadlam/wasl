Assignment: Follow Up on Missed Call
Automatically send a follow-up message to Contacts who called but reached no one, and assign the conversation to the right agent. Every missed call gets a response — without manual effort.
Benefits
  • Re-engage Contacts before they reach out to a competitor.
  • Automatically assign every missed call to an agent for timely follow-up.
  • Stop tracking missed calls manually.
How It Works
  1. A contact's incoming call goes unanswered.
  2. A new conversation is opened in respond.io.
  3. The conversation is assigned to the relevant agent or team.
  4. A follow-up message is sent to the contact.
